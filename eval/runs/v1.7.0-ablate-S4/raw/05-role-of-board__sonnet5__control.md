With operations now managed by divisional heads, the Board is free to focus fully on policy and planning—but doing so effectively requires resolving how the Board itself should be structured and run. This memorandum proposes ideas on four questions:

1. What size and composition would best suit the Board?
2. How should responsibilities be divided between the Board and the Executive Committee?
3. How can outside directors be made effective participants?
4. What principles should govern the selection and tenure of directors?

It also outlines alternative ways to move from the current arrangement to the one recommended.
