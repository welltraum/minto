## Audit findings

**1. The opening states structure, not an idea.**
The opening sentence tells the reader what the memo *does* and how it is *organized* ("organized in six sections"), but never states the governing thought — the single answer the document exists to deliver. A Minto introduction should establish Situation–Complication–Question and then give the Answer up front. Here the reader learns the document's table of contents, not its message.

**2. The six headings are topics, not ideas.**
Every heading names a *category* of information ("Background", "Principles", "Definition", "Organization", "Benefits", "Conditions") rather than asserting a point. Minto's rule: headings in the key line must express complete ideas so that a reader scanning only the headings can grasp the argument. Scanned alone, these six headings communicate nothing about what the project-team approach is or why the reader should care — they could head almost any management memo.

**3. No logical grouping or order is visible among the key-line points.**
Because the headings are topics, there is no way to test (and no evidence of) MECE grouping or a defensible deductive/inductive order. "Background", "Definition", "Organization", "Benefits", "Conditions" is a checklist, not a pyramid: nothing indicates which point is primary, how the points support one governing thought, or why they appear in this sequence.

**4. "Background" does not belong in the key line.**
Background/context material belongs in the introduction (the Situation), not as a structural section parallel to the argument's main points. Its presence as section one signals the document is organized by subject matter rather than by reasoning.

**5. The section count is announced rather than earned.**
"It is organized in six sections" reflects the writer's drafting plan, not the reader's need. In a pyramid-structured memo, the number of key points follows from the logic of the governing thought; announcing the count up front is a symptom of a document built top-down from headings instead of from an answer.

*Scope note: the body is unavailable, so this audit is confined to the visible opening and setout; whether the sections themselves contain pyramid logic cannot be assessed.*
