**Audit Result**

The opening violates the Minto Pyramid Principle in two ways:

1. **Missing main message**: The opening states only what the document *contains* ("describes how project teams identify and deliver significant profit improvements"), not the key insight or answer that justifies these six sections. Under Minto, the main point should be stated first, followed by supporting structure.

2. **Inverted logic**: The six-section list is presented as the answer to the document's purpose, rather than as support for an explicitly stated key message. The reader is asked to accept that these sections logically follow from the opening statement, but no connecting logic is made clear.
