**Introduction**

In October we reorganized so that divisional managers now have full authority for day-to-day operations. That leaves policy and planning squarely with the Board — yet for years the Board has spent most of its time dealing with short-term operational problems. If the Board is to carry out its proper role, we must decide how it should be organized and how it should work. The purpose of this memorandum is to collect ideas for discussion on the questions this raises.

**Points for discussion**

1. Board composition and optimum size.
2. The broad roles of the Board and Executive Committee, the responsibilities of each, and their relationship.
3. How to make an outside director an effective participant.
4. Principles for selecting directors and setting their tenure.
5. Alternative ways to move from the present Board and Executive Committee arrangement to the desired one.
