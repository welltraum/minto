### Three fixes that buy the most

1. Open with the answer to the reader's real question (what adopting the approach is worth), framed by a short SCQ intro — delete the statement of purpose.
2. Promote "benefits and results" into the key line: make the first-level groups the payoffs, ordered by ranking, and hang principles/organization/definition under them as supports.
3. Cut "Background" and "Definition" from the first level — fold the minimum necessary context into the intro, the rest into an appendix.
