Subject: TTW Composition Costs

TTW should reduce composition costs through two initiatives: (1) testing simplified processes for inexpensive titles, and (2) implementing productivity improvements in manual composition.

**Why this matters:**
Composition is TTW's largest production cost, accounting for 40% of hardback and 50–55% of paperback costs. TTW is uncompetitive due to below-benchmark productivity and high costs. The composing department is severely constrained: manual composition lacks capacity, recent staff losses have slowed operations, and overtime runs 50% over budget.

**Simplifying processes can save approximately 10% of composing costs.**
Currently, all titles—regardless of complexity—pass through identical quality-control stages. TTW should test whether selected inexpensive titles require fewer or differently timed checks. This testing will measure the actual effect on quality and customers before broader implementation.

**Productivity improvements are available.**
TTW trails industry benchmarks by 20–50%, indicating substantial opportunity to reduce costs through changes to the composing method. A formal methods study can identify specific improvements.

**Managers are willing to investigate both approaches.**
