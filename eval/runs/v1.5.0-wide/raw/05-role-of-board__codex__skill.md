The Board should refocus on policy and planning by defining its membership, governance model, outside-director role, and transition plan.

Since the reorganization gave divisional managers full authority for day-to-day operations, the Board’s responsibility is now policy and planning. Yet the Board has continued to spend substantial time on short-term operational problems. What changes are needed to establish the Board’s intended role?

The discussion will address:

1. **Board membership:** the Board’s optimum size, composition, director selection, and tenure.

2. **Governance responsibilities:** the respective roles of the Board and Executive Committee, and their working relationship.

3. **Outside-director participation:** how outside directors can contribute effectively.

4. **Transition:** alternative ways to move from the current Board and Executive Committee arrangement to the desired one.
