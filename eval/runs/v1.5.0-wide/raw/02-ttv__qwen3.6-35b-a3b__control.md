Subject: Recommendations to reduce TTW composing costs

TTW should reduce composing costs by simplifying the process for inexpensive titles and changing the composing method to increase productivity.

**Simplifying the process for inexpensive titles will save approximately 10% of total composing costs.**
*   Currently, every title undergoes identical quality-control stages regardless of complexity, creating unnecessary work for simple novels.
*   Testing selected jobs with fewer or differently timed checks can verify savings without compromising quality or customer satisfaction.
*   George Kennedy has agreed to conduct these tests.

**Changing the composing method is necessary to close a significant productivity gap.**
*   TTW's productivity trails the industry benchmark by 20–50%, indicating systemic inefficiencies.
*   A separate methods study is required to identify specific improvements.
*   Manual composition is currently overloaded, leading to job delays and overtime costs exceeding budget by over 50%.

**Cost reduction is urgent given the strategic weight of composition and current competitive position.**
*   Composition represents 40% of hardback and 50–55% of paperback costs.
*   TTW is uncompetitive on simple work, where process simplification offers the quickest relief.
*   All managers are willing to investigate these changes, ensuring organizational support for implementation.
