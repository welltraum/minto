Although the October reorganization gave divisional managers full operational authority and reserved policy and planning for the Board, the Board has been heavily involved in short-term operational problems. To enable it to focus on its proper role, we must address the following issues:

1. Optimal Board composition and size
2. The division of responsibilities between the Board and Executive Committee
3. How to ensure effective participation by outside directors
4. Principles for director selection and tenure
5. Alternative ways to move from the present Board and Executive Committee arrangement to the desired one
