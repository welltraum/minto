This memorandum recommends restructuring the Board to refocus its efforts on policy and planning, consistent with the October reorganization that vested divisional managers with full authority over day-to-day operations.

Although the reorganization clarified operational authority, the Board has continued to address short-term operational problems. To correct this misalignment, the following issues require resolution:

1. **Board Composition:** Determining the optimum size, membership criteria, and tenure for directors.
2. **Division of Roles:** Defining the responsibilities of the Board versus the Executive Committee and their relationship.
3. **Director Effectiveness:** Establishing mechanisms to ensure effective participation by outside directors.
4. **Transition Strategy:** Evaluating alternatives for moving from the current arrangement to the desired structure.
