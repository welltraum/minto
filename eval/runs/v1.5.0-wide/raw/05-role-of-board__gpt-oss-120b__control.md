**To align governance with the recent re‑organization, the Board must be reshaped so that it provides strategic oversight while delegating day‑to‑day operations to divisional managers. Accomplishing this requires us to address five inter‑related areas:**  

- **Board composition and optimal size** – defining the right mix of internal and external members.  
- **Division of responsibilities between the Board and the Executive Committee** – clarifying each body’s role and how they interact.  
- **Effective participation of outside directors** – ensuring external members can contribute meaningfully.  
- **Selection criteria and tenure policies for directors** – establishing transparent principles for appointment and length of service.  
- **Transition plan** – outlining steps to move from the current arrangement to the desired governance structure.
