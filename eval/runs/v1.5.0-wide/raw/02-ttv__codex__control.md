Subject: Reducing TTW composing costs

TTW should begin two focused tests to reduce composing costs: simplify quality control for inexpensive titles and redesign the composing method to improve productivity.

These actions address the two most credible sources of savings:

1. TTW applies essentially the same quality-control process to simple and complex titles.

   Test selected inexpensive titles with fewer or differently timed checks, and measure effects on quality and customer acceptance. This could reduce total composing cost by up to 10%.

2. TTW’s composing productivity trails industry benchmarks by 20–50%.

   Conduct a methods study to identify changes to the composing process that could close this gap, beginning with the capacity-constrained manual-composition work.

Management supports investigating both issues. George Kennedy is willing to lead tests of removable stages and the productivity gap.

These may explain operational pressure but do not directly support the recommended cost-reduction tests.
