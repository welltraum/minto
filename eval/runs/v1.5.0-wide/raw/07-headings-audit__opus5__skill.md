## Findings

| Location | Violation | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Opening sentence | No answer anywhere in the visible document — and the kind that belongs there is missing too: a reader deciding whether to commission project teams needs the reasons to do so (what they get, at what cost), not a description of how the approach works | Replace with the one-sentence answer the body supports, e.g. a recommendation with its payoff |
| Opening sentence | Statement of purpose in place of an introduction: no Situation, no Complication, no Question | Tell the story — what the reader already accepts, what changed, then the question the memo closes |
| Setout, all six | Mixed kinds in one group: principles, a definition, mechanics, benefits, conditions. No plural noun covers them | Re-derive the kind from the reader's question, then refill; make benefits the first level and demote mechanics under them |
| Setout, all six | Six first-level elements, no explicable order — not time, not structure, not ranking | Collapse to 3–4 and name the order |
| "Definition of project work" vs "Principles" / "Organization" | ⊗ Overlap: the same content can be filed under any of the three | Merge into one branch, or redraw the boundary |
| "Background" | Container heading; abstraction level does not match the key line; likely holds the Situation that belongs in the prose intro | Cut as a heading; fold its content into the introduction |
| "Distinctive benefits and results" | ↑ The reader's answer is buried in position five | Promote to the top and to the first branch |
