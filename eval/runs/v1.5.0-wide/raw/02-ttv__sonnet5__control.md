# TTW Composing Costs — Recommendation to Investigate Cost Reduction

**To:**
**From:**
**Subject:** TTW composing costs — recommendation for a two-part study

We recommend TTW launch a two-part study to reduce composing costs, since composition is TTW's largest production cost (40% of hardback, 50-55% of paperback) and industry comparisons suggest weak productivity and high cost relative to competitors.

## We recommend TTW pursue two actions:

**1. Simplify the quality-control process for inexpensive titles.**
Every title—complex reference book or simple novel—currently passes through the same quality-control stages. We recommend testing selected jobs with fewer or differently timed checks, measuring the effect on quality and customer satisfaction. Potential saving: up to 10% of total composing cost.

**2. Commission a methods study to raise productivity.**
TTW's measured productivity trails the industry benchmark by 20-50%. George Kennedy has agreed to test whether stages can be removed and to investigate the cause of the shortfall.

## Supporting context

TTW is uncompetitive on simple work and does not yet know whether its composing costs are genuinely excessive relative to what the work requires — these two actions are designed to answer that question and act on it.
