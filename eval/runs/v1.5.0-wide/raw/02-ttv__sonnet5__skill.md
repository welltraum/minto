# Fixture 02 — Rewritten

Subject: Two tests to find where composing costs can be cut

Situation: Composition is TTW's largest production cost — roughly 40% of hardback and 50-55% of paperback cost — and TTW is uncompetitive on simple work, but we don't yet know how much of that cost is genuinely excessive.

Complication: The composing room applies the same quality-control stages to every title, simple or complex, and is running below the productivity benchmark by 20-50% while overloaded, understaffed, and paying overtime more than 50% over budget.

Answer: We recommend two tests to locate where composing cost can actually be cut.

1. **Test a simplified quality-control process on simple titles.** Run selected simple jobs through fewer, differently-timed checks and measure the effect on quality and customers. Potential saving: up to 10% of total composing cost.

2. **Run a methods study of the composing process.** TTW trails the productivity benchmark by 20-50%; a methods study would identify which changes to the composing method close that gap. George Kennedy is willing to run both this study and the quality-control test.

What we need now: sign-off from Aylesbury to begin both tests, with Kennedy as the point of contact.
