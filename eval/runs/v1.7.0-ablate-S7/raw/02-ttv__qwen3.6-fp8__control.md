# Fixture 02: TTW Composing Costs (Restructured)

**To:** Engagement Manager  
**From:** Consultant  
**Subject:** Recommendation: Reduce Composing Costs by Simplifying Processes and Improving Productivity  

**Recommendation**  
I recommend that TTW reduce composing costs by simplifying quality control for simple titles and conducting a methods study to address significant productivity gaps.

**1. Simplify quality control for simple titles**  
*   **Potential saving:** Reducing checks for inexpensive titles could save up to 10% of total composing costs.  
*   **Current inefficiency:** All titles, from complex reference books to simple novels, currently pass through identical quality-control stages.  
*   **Action:** Test selected jobs with fewer or differently timed checks to measure the effect on quality and customers. George Kennedy has agreed to lead this test.

**2. Improve productivity through method changes**  
*   **Performance gap:** TTW's measured productivity trails industry benchmarks by 20–50%, indicating substantial opportunity for improvement.  
*   **Operational strain:** The department is overloaded, resulting in overtime exceeding budget by 50%, missed deadlines, and difficulty retaining staff due to below-market pay rates.  
*   **Action:** Commission a methods study to identify specific productivity gains. Management is united in its willingness to investigate solutions.

**Background**  
*   Composition accounts for approximately 40% of hardback costs and 50–55% of paperback costs.  
*   Industry comparisons confirm weak productivity at TTW relative to competitors.
