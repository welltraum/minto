# TTW Composing Costs

**To:** [Aylesbury management]
**From:** [Engagement manager]
**Subject:** Reducing composing-room costs

We recommend TTW pursue two independent actions to reduce composing costs, which represent 40% of hardback and 50-55% of paperback production cost: simplify quality-control on inexpensive titles, and improve productivity in manual composition.

## 1. Simplify the process for inexpensive titles

Every title currently passes through the same quality-control stages regardless of complexity — a simple novel gets the same checks as a complex reference book. We recommend TTW test selected jobs with fewer or differently timed checks, measuring the effect on quality and customer response. The potential saving is roughly 10% of total composing cost. George Kennedy has agreed to run this test.

## 2. Improve productivity in manual composition

Manual composition is TTW's most capacity-constrained area and trails the industry benchmark by 20-50%. Contributing factors:

- TTW pays below local market rates, making compositors hard to hire and retain; two have recently left.
- The department is overloaded, jobs are running late, and overtime is running more than 50% over budget.
- A union pay claim is pending.

We recommend a separate methods study, also to be run by Kennedy, to identify specific productivity improvements and quantify the achievable saving.

## Next steps

- Kennedy tests reduced/adjusted quality checks on a sample of simple titles (2-3 weeks).
- Kennedy leads a methods study of manual composition productivity.
- Findings and savings estimates to be reported jointly once both are complete.
