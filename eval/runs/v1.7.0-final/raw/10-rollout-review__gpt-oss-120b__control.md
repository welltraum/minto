**FIELDBOOK ROLLOUT – TWO‑QUARTER REVIEW  
Decision Brief for the COO**  

---

### 1. Recommendation  
**Approve extension of Fieldbook to the North and Islands in Q1, provided that**  

1. **On‑boarding is re‑designed** – a repeatable, multi‑session classroom plus on‑demand e‑learning path.  
2. **Critical integrations are delivered before go‑live** – asset‑register feed and job‑code mapping.  
3. **A monitoring gate is added at week‑4** to capture early field signals and trigger corrective actions.  

If these conditions are met, projected ROI matches the Central/East experience and the extension will be financially positive within the first two quarters.

---

### 2. Rationale – Three Pillars that Support the Recommendation  

| Pillar | Core Insight | Evidence (Q1‑Q2) |
|--------|--------------|-----------------|
| **A. Adoption can be replicated** | Full adoption is achievable when training is timely and repeatable. | • Central & East reached 100 % technician activation and 83 % order capture within 6 weeks. <br>• West lagged (71 % after 9 weeks) because only one classroom session was offered; 34/118 technicians never attended. |
| **B. Operational & financial upside outweighs incremental cost** | Scheduling density, travel‑time reduction and overtime savings generate net cash‑flow positive results when adoption ≥ 80 %. | • Jobs/tech/day rose from 4.6 → 5.3 (≈ 15 % uplift). <br>• Travel time fell 18 %; fuel cost per job down 9 %. <br>• Overtime ↓ 12 % QoQ, offsetting £31k/mo licence cost after Q2 in high‑adoption regions. |
| **C. Integration gaps are known, vendor‑supported, and low‑effort** | No technical blockers; only internal ownership and scheduling are missing. | • Asset‑register export and job‑code mapping are documented connectors; vendor estimates ≤ 1 day to configure each. <br>• Current manual re‑keying costs £8.4k/quarter and creates 2‑4 % reporting variance. |

When the three pillars are satisfied, the extension’s incremental licence (£13.5k/mo) is covered by the same per‑tech efficiency gains observed in Central/East.

---

### 3. Detailed Findings (MECE)  

#### 3.1 Adoption  
- **Live regions:** Central, East, South, West (4/6).  
- **Technician activation:** 412/486 (85 %).  
- **Order capture:** 83 % of work orders entered in Fieldbook.  
- **Training shortfall:** Single 2‑day classroom per region; new hires and absentees missed it → reliance on peer shadowing (34 % of West technicians never attended).  

#### 3.2 Operations  
- **Scheduling density:** +0.7 jobs/tech/day (15 % increase).  
- **Travel time:** –18 % per day.  
- **Emergency insertion:** Median 4 min vs. manual phone chain.  
- **Defects:** <br>• No asset‑history feed → 9 % of jobs duplicate warranty parts. <br>• Job‑code mismatch → 300 manual re‑keys/week, invoicing errors.  

#### 3.3 Support & Training  
- **Tickets:** 1,940 total; peak at week‑3, then decline.  
- **Ticket mix:** 44 % password/login (mostly untrained techs); 19 % duplicate‑customer records (search limited to exact postcode).  
- **Support workload:** 70 duplicate merges/week; ongoing billing‑code escalations.  
- **Current mitigation:** Weekly 1‑hr webinars (20‑30 attendees) – attendance aligns with regions lacking classroom slots.  

#### 3.4 Finance  
- **Running cost:** £31k/mo licence/hosting (4 regions).  
- **Savings:** Overtime ↓ 12 %; fuel ↓ 9 % (driven by scheduling gains).  
- **Unplanned costs:** <br>• Shadow‑training lost route time ≈ 380 hrs (South/West). <br>• Temporary billing clerk £8.4k/2 qtrs for manual re‑keying.  
- **Break‑even:** Central/East recouped costs by Q2; South/West at break‑even.  

#### 3.5 Data & Integrations  
- **Reporting:** Fieldbook is source of truth for job status.  
- **Data quality issues:** <br>• Customer duplicate records (≈ 70/week). <br>• Missing asset‑history panel (no warranty visibility). <br>• Job‑code mismatch → 2‑4 % variance in completed‑job counts.  
- **Vendor capability:** Connectors for asset register and invoicing exist; not yet scoped or owned.  

#### 3.6 Program Office Observations  
- Early warning signs (by week‑4) were not acted upon → repeated issues across regions.  
- Vendor relationship strong; solutions are waiting on Kestrel decisions, not on vendor delivery.  

---

### 4. Required Actions for Extension  

| Action | Owner | Target | Success Metric |
|--------|-------|--------|----------------|
| **Redesign onboarding** – multi‑session classroom + recorded e‑learning + “first‑login” support window. | Training Lead (HR) | Q1‑Week 1 | ≥ 80 % technician activation by week 6. |
| **Implement asset‑register export** – nightly feed into Fieldbook. | IT Integration Manager | Q1‑Week 2 | 0 % missing equipment history on job screens. |
| **Configure job‑code mapping** – one‑day vendor‑supported setup. | Finance Ops Lead | Q1‑Week 2 | Zero manual re‑keying after go‑live. |
| **Add week‑4 monitoring gate** – adoption, ticket volume, integration health; trigger corrective plan. | Program Office | Q1‑Week 4 (each region) | Issues resolved before week 8. |
| **Finalize licence budget** – add £13.5k/mo for North & Islands. | CFO | Q1‑Week 1 | Approved budget line. |
| **Communicate revised rollout plan** – to regional coordinators and dispatchers. | COO Office | Q1‑Week 1 | All stakeholders briefed; training calendar published. |

---

### 5. Bottom Line  

- **If the three conditions are met, the North and Islands rollout will replicate the Central/East ROI within two quarters, delivering net savings that exceed the additional £13.5k/mo licence cost.**  
- **Without these safeguards, the rollout risks the same adoption lag and cost overruns seen in West, which would erode financial benefits for at least the first two quarters.**  

**Decision:** Approve extension **contingent** on the above action plan.
