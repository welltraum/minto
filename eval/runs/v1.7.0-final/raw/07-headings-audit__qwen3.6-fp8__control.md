**Audit Findings**

- **Violates “Answer-First” Principle:** The opening leads with a descriptive purpose statement (“This memorandum describes…”) rather than stating the main conclusion, recommendation, or direct answer to the reader’s implicit question.
- **Missing Logical Sequencing Framework:** The six headings are presented as a flat list without indicating the underlying logic governing their order (e.g., chronological, structural, causal, or degree-based). MPP requires that supporting ideas follow a single, explicit reasoning path.
- **No Evidence of Top-Down Grouping:** The setout shows no hierarchical clustering or subordination. MPP mandates that related concepts be grouped under higher-level themes before being enumerated.
- **Incomplete Contextual Framing:** The opening lacks a clear statement of scope, boundary conditions, or the specific business question/decision being addressed, which weakens the necessary setup for an answer-first structure.
- **Inconsistent Conceptual Level Across Headings:** The phrasing mixes methodological elements (“Principles,” “Definition,” “Organization”) with outcome-oriented elements (“Benefits and results”) and prerequisites (“Conditions”). This suggests a lack of mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive categorization at the outline stage, violating MPP’s grouping discipline.
