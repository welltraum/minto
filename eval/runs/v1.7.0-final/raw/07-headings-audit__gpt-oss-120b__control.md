**Audit Findings**

1. **Missing top‑level message** – The opening does not state a clear conclusion, recommendation, or key insight; it merely describes the memorandum’s purpose, which violates the Minto rule that the first paragraph should present the answer to the reader’s question.

2. **Purpose‑statement instead of answer** – “This memorandum describes …” is a purpose statement, not the answer that the reader is seeking. The opening should articulate *what* the reader should take away, not just that the document exists.

3. **Absence of a logical hierarchy** – The list of six sections is presented as a flat enumeration rather than a hierarchical structure that shows how the sections support the top‑level message.

4. **Parallelism and MECE issues in headings**  
   - The headings are not uniformly phrased (e.g., “Background” vs. “Principles of the project‑team approach” vs. “Conditions required for success”).  
   - Potential overlap between “Distinctive benefits and results” and “Conditions required for success” may breach the MECE principle (mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive).

5. **No preview of the argument flow** – The opening does not indicate the logical progression (e.g., situation → complication → question → answer) that should guide the reader through the memorandum.
