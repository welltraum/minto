**Subject:** Recommend next sprint focus: prioritize defect reduction

**Situation:** 
Our backlog is growing as new defects consistently outpace our closure rate.

**Complication:** 
Projections show the gap will widen across the year. More urgently, the backlog is now creating measurable business impact: CX data links defect volume to customer churn, Sales reports that defect concerns are blocking deals, and a major account has escalated concerns. This is no longer a technical issue—it's a revenue and credibility issue.

**Answer:** 
We should prioritize defect reduction in the next sprint.

**Why:**
1. **We prevent customer churn.** CX research directly ties defect volume to retention loss. Each sprint we delay, we lose more customers and the revenue we'd spend months recovering.

2. **We unblock the sales pipeline.** Defect concerns are blocking deals—a major account has escalated, and reps report deal impact. Fixing defects restores credibility and unblocks prospects.

3. **We prevent the backlog from becoming unmanageable.** New defects now outpace closures every period. Without intervention, we spiral toward crisis mode where we can only patch emergencies.

**What I need now:**
Sign-off so we can brief the team and finalize the sprint plan.
