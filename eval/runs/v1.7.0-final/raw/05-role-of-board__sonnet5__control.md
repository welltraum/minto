The Board should shift its focus from operational matters to policy and planning, but doing so raises five questions that need to be resolved first:

1. What size and composition will let the Board carry out its planning and policy role effectively?
2. How should responsibilities be divided between the Board and the Executive Committee, and how should the two bodies relate to one another?
3. How can outside directors be made effective participants?
4. What principles should govern the selection and tenure of directors?
5. How should the Board move from its present arrangement with the Executive Committee to the arrangement it wants?
