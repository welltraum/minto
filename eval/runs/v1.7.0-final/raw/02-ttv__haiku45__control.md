Subject: Reducing Composition Costs at TTW

**Recommendation**

TTW should pursue two parallel initiatives to reduce composition costs: (1) simplify quality-control procedures for simple titles, targeting ~10% cost savings, and (2) conduct a methods study to close the 20-50% productivity gap against industry benchmarks.

**Process Simplification**

All titles currently pass through identical quality-control stages regardless of complexity. Testing simplified procedures on selected simple titles will reveal whether fewer or differently-timed checks can reduce costs while maintaining quality and customer satisfaction.

**Productivity Improvement**

TTW's manual composition productivity trails the industry benchmark by 20-50%. A methods study can identify improvements to close this gap. Management is willing to undertake such an investigation.

**Why These Initiatives Matter**

Composition represents 40% of hardback and 50-55% of paperback production costs. TTW is uncompetitive on simple work, making these cost reductions urgent.
