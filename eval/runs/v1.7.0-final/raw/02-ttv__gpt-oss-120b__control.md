**To:** Engagement Manager – TTW Project  
**From:** Junior Consultant – Composing‑Room Review  
**Subject:** Action‑Oriented Recommendation to Reduce TTW Composing Costs  

---

### Recommendation  
**Pilot a simplified composition process for low‑complexity titles and test targeted productivity‑enhancing changes.**  
If the pilot confirms the expected 10 %‑15 % reduction in composing cost, roll the changes out across the department to achieve a total cost saving of roughly 5 %–7 % of overall hard‑back and paperback production costs.

---

### Supporting Arguments  

1. **Composition dominates production cost and TTW lags the industry.**  
   * Composition accounts for ~40 % of hard‑back and 50‑55 % of paperback cost.  
   * Benchmark data show TTW’s measured productivity is 20 %–50 % below comparable printers, indicating excess cost rather than a pricing issue.

2. **A focused simplification can deliver measurable savings.**  
   * Reducing or re‑timing quality‑control checks on inexpensive titles is projected to cut composing cost by up to 10 % for those titles.  
   * Changing the composing method (e.g., increasing automation, revising workflow) has the potential to close the 20 %–50 % productivity gap.

3. **The department’s capacity constraints make a pilot essential.**  
   * Manual composition is already overloaded; overtime exceeds budget by >50 %.  
   * Recent staff turnover (two compositors lost) and difficulty hiring at below‑market rates limit the ability to implement broad changes without first proving impact.

4. **A structured pilot provides the evidence needed for a full rollout.**  
   * Select a representative sample of low‑complexity titles (e.g., novels, short non‑technical works).  
   * Measure cost, cycle‑time, and quality before and after removing/re‑sequencing specific checks.  
   * Use the results to quantify savings, assess quality impact, and refine the new workflow before scaling.
