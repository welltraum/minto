Subject: Recommendation: Dedicate next sprint to reducing defect backlog

As we plan the next sprint, our incoming defect rate exceeds our fix rate, projecting a widening backlog that is actively harming retention and revenue. Customer data links this backlog directly to increased churn, while a major account complaint and falling sales close rates signal immediate pipeline damage. To arrest this trend, we must dedicate the next sprint to reducing the defect backlog.

**Why this is critical:**

*   **The backlog is driving churn.** Churn correlates with defect volume, reinforced by user posts and interview feedback showing severe customer frustration.
*   **The backlog is eroding sales velocity.** A major account has lodged a formal complaint, and Sales reports falling close rates attributed to these quality concerns.
*   **The backlog is accelerating.** Incoming defects consistently outpace fixes, projecting a worsening trajectory that compounds the risk daily.

**Decision needed:** Approve the next sprint focus as defect reduction.
