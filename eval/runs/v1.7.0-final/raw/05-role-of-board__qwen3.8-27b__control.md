Following the October reorganization, which gave divisional managers full authority over day-to-day operations, the Board should concentrate on policy and planning rather than short-term operational problems. To define that role, this memorandum sets out three areas for discussion:

1. **Mandate and division of work**
   - The Board’s core responsibilities for policy and planning.
   - The Executive Committee’s responsibilities and the relationship between the two bodies.

2. **Composition and membership**
   - The Board’s optimum size and membership.
   - Principles for selecting directors and setting their tenure.
   - How outside directors can participate effectively.

3. **Transition**
   - Options for moving from the present Board and Executive Committee arrangements to the desired model.
