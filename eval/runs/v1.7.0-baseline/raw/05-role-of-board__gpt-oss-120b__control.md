**Key Message**  
To align governance with the recent re‑organization, we must define a board structure that (1) is the right size and composition, (2) clearly separates strategic responsibilities of the Board from the operational duties of the Executive Committee, (3) enables outside directors to contribute effectively, (4) establishes transparent principles for selecting directors and determining their tenure, and (5) outlines a practical transition from the current arrangement to the desired model.

**Points for discussion**  

1. **Board composition and optimum size** – identifying the appropriate number of members and the mix of internal and external directors.  
2. **Roles and responsibilities** – clarifying the strategic remit of the Board versus the day‑to‑day operational role of the Executive Committee and how the two bodies interact.  
3. **Effective participation of outside directors** – ensuring external members can add value and influence decisions.  
4. **Selection criteria and tenure principles** – setting transparent standards for appointing directors and defining the length and renewal of their terms.  
5. **Transition pathway** – proposing alternative approaches to move from the present Board/Executive Committee structure to the target governance model.
