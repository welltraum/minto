The October reorganization transferred operational authority to divisional managers while designating policy and planning as the Board's domain. Yet the Board continues to manage operational issues, creating ambiguity about its intended role and structure. To resolve this and enable effective governance, we need to address four core issues:

1. Board composition and optimum size.
2. The respective roles and responsibilities of the Board and Executive Committee.
3. How to make outside directors effective participants.
4. Principles for director selection and tenure.
