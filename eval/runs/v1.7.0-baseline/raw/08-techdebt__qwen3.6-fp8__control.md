Subject: Proposal: Dedicate Next Sprint to Reducing Defect Backlog

Hi Alex,

I recommend dedicating the next sprint to reducing the defect backlog. This intervention is necessary to halt the accelerating technical debt and mitigate its impact on customer retention and revenue.

*   **The backlog is growing unsustainably.** Incoming defects now exceed the number we fix. A one-year projection indicates the backlog will continue to expand without dedicated remediation.
*   **Defects are driving churn.** Customer experience data correlates rising defect volume with increased churn. Qualitative interviews confirm that instability is degrading the user experience.
*   **Brand and sales are at risk.** We have received a complaint from a major account and observe negative sentiment in user posts. Sales reports that these quality concerns are lowering close rates.

Allocating the sprint to this work will stabilize the product and protect our growth metrics. Please let me know if you support this direction.
