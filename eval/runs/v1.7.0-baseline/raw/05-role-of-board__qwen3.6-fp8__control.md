To refocus the Board on policy and planning, we propose restructuring its composition and clarifying its relationship with the Executive Committee. This memorandum addresses:

1.  **Optimizing Board composition.** Recommendations include determining the optimum size, establishing principles for selecting directors and setting their tenure, and defining how to ensure effective participation by outside members.
2.  **Clarifying the division of work.** Proposals for defining the broad roles, responsibilities, and relationship between the Board and the Executive Committee.
3.  **Selecting a transition path.** Evaluation of alternative methods to move from the current Board and Executive Committee arrangement to the desired one.
