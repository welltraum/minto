**Fieldbook Rollout – Two‑Quarter Review  
Decision Brief for the COO (Q1 Extension)**  

---

### 1. Recommendation  
**Approve extension of Fieldbook to the North and Islands in Q1 2025**, **provided that** the onboarding process is re‑designed to replicate the rapid‑adoption model used in Central and East.  

*Rationale*: The rollout has already delivered measurable operational and financial benefits in the four live regions. Those benefits will be realized in the remaining regions only if adoption speed and data‑integration gaps are addressed up‑front.  

---

### 2. Summary of Evidence (MECE)

| Area | What the data show | Implication for extension |
|------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| **Adoption** | • 85 % (412/486) technicians activated accounts.<br>• Central & East reached 80 % order‑in‑system within 6 weeks; South took 11 weeks, West still at 71 % after 9 weeks.<br>• Dispatchers 100 % on‑board from day 1. | Fast adoption drives ROI. The two‑day classroom was the bottleneck in slower regions. A repeat‑able, multi‑session training plan is essential. |
| **Operations** | • Jobs/tech/day ↑ 15 % (4.6 → 5.3).<br>• Travel time ↓ 18 %.<br>• Emergency insertions now 4 min median.<br>• Defects: missing asset‑history feed (9 % of jobs duplicate parts) and mismatched completion codes (≈300 re‑keys/week). | Scheduling gains offset by data gaps. Fixes (asset‑register export, code‑mapping) are vendor‑ready; must be scoped before go‑live. |
| **Support & Training** | • 1,940 tickets/2 qtrs; 44 % password resets, 19 % duplicate‑customer records.<br>• Ticket volume peaks at week 3, then declines.<br>• Weekly 1‑hr webinars reach only 20‑30 techs (mainly those who missed classroom). | High ticket volume correlates with missed classroom training. Structured, repeatable classroom + supplemental webinars will reduce support load. |
| **Finance** | • Licence/hosting £31 k/mo (4 regions).<br>• Overtime ↓ 12 %; fuel cost/job ↓ 9 % (directly linked to scheduling gains).<br>• Unplanned costs: 380 hrs lost to shadowing; £8.4 k for temporary billing clerk.<br>• Break‑even reached in Q2 for Central/East; South/West at breakeven. | Net financial benefit materialises when adoption ≥ 80 % within 6 weeks. Extension adds £13.5 k/mo licences; ROI positive if same adoption curve is achieved. |
| **Data & Integrations** | • Reporting now source‑of‑record; but:<br> – Customer‑record duplicates (≈70 /week) split history.<br> – No asset‑history feed (warranty visibility lost).<br> – Completion‑code mismatch → 2‑4 % reporting variance.<br>• Vendor provides connectors for both asset register and invoicing; none have been scoped. | Data integrity issues are technical, not cultural. Scoping and implementing the two connectors before go‑live eliminates the biggest operational friction. |
| **Program Office Observations** | • Early‑stage issues (training gaps, integration gaps) were visible by week 4 in Central but were not escalated.<br>• Vendor relationship strong; solutions exist, awaiting Kestrel decisions. | Governance change needed: early‑field observations → program‑level corrective actions. This will prevent repeat‑of‑West pattern. |

---

### 3. Key Risks & Mitigations  

| Risk | Impact if unmitigated | Mitigation (to be enacted before Q1 go‑live) |
|------|----------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| **Slow technician onboarding** (as in West) | Low adoption → negative ROI, higher support cost. | • Deploy a **tiered classroom schedule** (minimum 2 sessions per region, plus on‑demand webinars).<br>• Assign a **regional onboarding lead** accountable for 80 % activation within 6 weeks. |
| **Missing asset‑history feed** | Duplicate part replacements, warranty errors. | • Prioritize the **asset‑register export connector** (nightly feed).<br>• Validate in a pilot job before full rollout. |
| **Completion‑code mismatch** | Manual re‑keying → labor cost, invoice errors. | • Implement the vendor’s **code‑mapping table** (1‑day setup).<br>• Assign a **data‑owner** to maintain the mapping. |
| **Customer‑record duplication** | Fragmented job history, support tickets. | • Enhance the search algorithm (postcode + address).<br>• Run a **data‑clean‑up** sprint before go‑live; lock duplicate creation. |
| **Governance gap (early issue escalation)** | Repeated patterns across regions. | • Create a **Rapid‑Feedback Loop**: weekly regional health score → program office steering committee. |
| **Winter travel for classroom training** | Delayed onboarding, higher logistics cost. | • Use **regional “train‑the‑trainer”** model: certify local super‑users who can run classroom sessions on‑site, reducing travel. |

---

### 4. Implementation Blueprint for North & Islands  

| Phase | Owner | Deliverable | Timeline |
|-------|-------|--------------|----------|
| **1. Planning & Governance** | Program Office | Revised onboarding charter; rapid‑feedback process defined. | Weeks 1‑2 |
| **2. Technical Integration** | IT & Vendor | Asset‑register export connector; completion‑code mapping table; enhanced search. | Weeks 2‑4 |
| **3. Training Rollout** | Regional Coordinators | 2 classroom sessions per region + weekly webinars; local super‑user certification. | Weeks 3‑6 |
| **4. Go‑Live & Support** | Support Desk | Dedicated “early‑adoption” support window (first 4 weeks), ticket triage plan. | Week 6 onward |
| **5. Review & Adjust** | COO & Finance | KPI check (adoption %, tickets, cost savings) at week 8; decision on further scaling. | Week 8 |

*Key KPIs to hit by week 6*: ≥ 80 % of technicians active; ≤ 20 % ticket volume relative to baseline; asset‑history feed live; code‑mapping operational.

---

### 5. Bottom Line  

- **Financially**: Extension adds £13.5 k/mo licence cost but is projected to become cash‑positive within two quarters **if** adoption mirrors Central/East.  
- **Operationally**: Scheduling gains and reduced travel fuel already delivering measurable savings; data‑integration fixes will unlock further efficiency.  
- **Strategically**: Uniform Fieldbook usage across all six regions will standardize work‑order visibility, improve customer service, and position Kestrel for future digital initiatives.  

**Decision**: Approve Q1 extension **contingent on** execution of the onboarding and integration mitigations outlined above.
