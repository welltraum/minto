Subject: TTW Composing Costs

TTW should reduce composing costs by testing simplified quality-control processes for simple titles and conducting a methods study to improve productivity across the department.

**Why This Matters**

Composition represents 40% of hardback and 50–55% of paperback production costs. Industry comparisons show TTW is uncompetitive on simple work, with weak productivity and costs high relative to the benchmark.

**Root Cause**

TTW applies identical quality-control stages to every title, regardless of complexity. The manual composition function is severely capacity-constrained: below-market wages have caused recent staff losses, hiring is difficult, and the department now runs jobs late with overtime exceeding budget by more than 50%.

**Recommended Actions**

1. **Test simplified processes for inexpensive titles.** Remove or reschedule quality-control checks for simple jobs and measure the effect on quality and customer satisfaction. This could reduce composing costs by up to 10%.

2. **Conduct a methods study.** TTW trails the benchmark by 20–50% in productivity, suggesting substantial room for improvement through process redesign.

**Feasibility**

Department managers are willing to investigate both initiatives.
