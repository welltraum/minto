# Audit Result

**Issues identified:**

1. **Logical sequencing violation**: "Conditions required for success" appears as the final section when it should logically appear earlier in the structure—conditions are prerequisites that should be established before discussing implementation and organization.

2. **Weak main idea statement**: The opening restates what the memorandum does ("describes how...it is organized in...") rather than asserting a key takeaway or recommendation. This reduces clarity of the central point.

3. **Unclear pyramid structure**: The six sections do not demonstrate a clear hierarchical relationship supporting a single main idea. The grouping appears sequential rather than logically pyramidal.
