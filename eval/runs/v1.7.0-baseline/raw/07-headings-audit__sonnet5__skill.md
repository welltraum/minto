## Findings

| Location | Violation | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Opening sentence | Top is a purpose statement ("describes how…"), not an answer. No reader question is ever posed or closed. | Determine the reader's real question (likely "should we adopt this approach / what will it get us") and state the answer as a claim, e.g. "Project teams deliver profit improvements of X through Y." |
| Six-item list overall | Not same-kind: mixes a topic label (Background), a method description (Principles, Definition, Organization), an outcome (Distinctive benefits and results), and a precondition (Conditions required for success). Fails the "one plural noun" test. | Re-derive the kind from the reader's question. If the question is "why adopt this," the first level should be reasons/benefits, not a table of contents of sections. |
| Ordering of the six headings | No explicit ordering principle (not time, not structure, not ranking, not deduction/induction) — reads as recall order. | Name and apply one order type once the correct kind of grouping is settled. |
| "Background" heading | Classic anti-pattern heading — sits at a different level of abstraction than the other five and answers nothing itself. | Fold into the story (Situation/Complication) as prose, not as a first-level branch. |
