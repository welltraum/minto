Since the reorganization in October transferred full authority for day-to-day operations to divisional managers, the Board is now free to devote its time to policy and planning, its proper role. To do this effectively, the Board must resolve four questions:

1. What size and composition will let the Board best perform its policy role?
2. How should work be divided between the Board and the Executive Committee, and how should the two bodies relate?
3. How can outside directors be made effective participants?
4. What principles should govern the selection and tenure of directors?

This memorandum sets out ideas on each question, and proposes how the Board might move from its present arrangement with the Executive Committee to the one recommended.
