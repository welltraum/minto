# TTW Composing Costs

**To:** [Aylesbury management]
**From:** [Engagement manager]
**Subject:** Reducing composing-room costs

## Recommendation

TTW should launch two immediate actions to cut composing costs, which account for 40% of hardback and 50-55% of paperback production cost:

1. **Simplify the process for inexpensive titles** — test reduced or restructured quality-control checks on selected jobs, potentially saving up to 10% of total composing cost.
2. **Commission a methods study to raise compositor productivity** — TTW's measured productivity trails the industry benchmark by 20-50%, and George Kennedy has agreed to test which stages can be removed and why the gap exists.

## Why This Matters

Every title, regardless of complexity, currently passes through the same quality-control stages. Simple work does not need the same checks as complex reference books, which is why TTW is uncompetitive on simple jobs. Testing fewer or differently timed checks will show how far checks can be cut without harming quality or customer satisfaction.

The productivity gap compounds the problem: TTW pays below local market rates, cannot hire or retain compositors, has just lost two employees, and now runs most jobs late. Overtime is over 50% over budget, and a new union claim adds pressure. A methods study addresses the root cause rather than the symptoms.

- Select pilot titles and design the reduced-checks test (owner: TBD).
- Scope the methods study with George Kennedy.
- Set a review date to assess pilot results before wider rollout.
