This memorandum recommends restructuring the Board so that it can concentrate on policy and planning rather than day-to-day operations. Specifically, it addresses:

1. Board composition and optimum size
2. The division of work between the Board and the Executive Committee
3. How to make an outside director an effective participant
4. Principles for selecting directors and setting their tenure
5. Alternative ways to move from the present Board and Executive Committee arrangement to the desired one
