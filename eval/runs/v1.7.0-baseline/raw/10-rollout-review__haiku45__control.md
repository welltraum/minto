# FIELDBOOK ROLLOUT — EXTENSION DECISION

## Recommendation

**Extend Fieldbook to North and Islands in Q1, conditional on restructuring onboarding and completing two system integrations before launch.**

---

## The case for extension is strong

### Operational gains are substantial and proven across all four regions

Fieldbook has delivered consistent improvements regardless of adoption speed:

- **Productivity:** Jobs per technician per day increased from 4.6 to 5.3 (+15%)
- **Efficiency:** Average travel time between jobs fell 18%
- **Emergency response:** Automatic routing achieves 4-minute insertion (previously manual phone-tag)

South and West show identical operational gains despite slower adoption, confirming the platform itself drives value.

### Financial case is favorable under realistic adoption scenarios

Central and East achieved payback within Q2. Extension to North and Islands would cost £13,500/month.

- If adoption matches Central/East (6 weeks to full adoption): payback in Q2; full-year net positive
- If adoption matches West (11 weeks to full adoption): payback in Q3; marginal but non-negative
- Cost of deferral to Q2: winter travel raises training costs; narrower recruitment window for pre-trained staff

---

## Three conditions must be met to avoid repeating South/West outcomes

### Condition 1: Restructure onboarding from classroom to flexible delivery

South and West suffered adoption lag because the single classroom session left 34 of 118 technicians in West unattended and forced 380 tech-hours of improvised shadowing to compensate.

The support desk's weekly webinar (20–30 attendees) proves that flexible, recurring training works. Replace one-time classroom with weekly webinars plus on-demand modules to accommodate shift patterns and late hires.

### Condition 2: Integrate the legacy asset register before launch

Technicians currently complete jobs blind to service history. Result: 9% of audited jobs re-replaced parts already under warranty within the past year. They compensate by calling the back office or asking customers, driving wasted time and support tickets.

The vendor connector is ready. IT estimates one day to implement. Integrate in Q1 month one.

### Condition 3: Configure invoicing code mapping before launch

Fieldbook's job-completion codes do not match the invoicing system's codes. The billing team manually re-keys roughly 300 records per week, introducing transcription errors. The vendor's mapping table exists and requires one day to configure. Assign integration ownership and configure in Q1 month one.

---

## Structural improvement needed

The program office noted that every negative pattern was visible by week four of Central's launch but had no mechanism to escalate field observations into program-level fixes. Each subsequent region replayed the same issues.

Implement weekly cross-regional issue escalation: one field representative from each live region plus the program office flags recurring issues for weekly resolution.

---

## Summary

Approve extension to North and Islands for Q1. Operational and financial cases justify it. The barriers that slowed South and West—all identified and fixable—must be addressed in Q1 months one and two. The cost of conditions is low; the cost of delay to Q2 (winter travel, recruitment timing, deferred benefit) is higher.
