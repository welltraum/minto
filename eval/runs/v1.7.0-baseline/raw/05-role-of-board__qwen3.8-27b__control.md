The Board should concentrate on policy and planning, leaving day-to-day operations to divisional managers. To do this, we must decide:

1. What the optimum size and composition of the Board should be.
2. How the work should be divided between the Board and the Executive Committee.
3. How outside directors can be made effective participants.
4. What principles should govern the selection and tenure of directors.
5. How the present Board and Executive Committee arrangement can be changed to the desired one.
