Subject: Reducing TTW composing costs

TTW should reduce composing costs through two focused actions: simplify the process for suitable low-cost titles and conduct a methods study to improve productivity.

TTW can likely reduce total composing cost by up to 10% by removing or retiming quality-control checks on selected simple jobs. Currently, simple novels and complex reference works pass through essentially the same control stages. TTW should test a revised process on selected titles and measure effects on quality and customer acceptance before wider implementation.

TTW should also investigate changes to the composing method. TTW’s measured productivity trails industry benchmarks by 20–50%, indicating scope for further savings beyond process simplification. A separate methods study should identify the causes and recommend practical improvements.

Management should proceed with these investigations now. Although managers disagree about whether TTW’s costs are excessive, they agree that the issue warrants examination and are willing to participate.

Omitted or demoted: staffing shortages, pay levels, turnover, late jobs, overtime, union claims, individual interview details, and comparisons with other printers. These facts may explain current operating pressure, but they do not establish or support the recommended cost-reduction programme.
