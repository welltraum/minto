**Introduction**

In October, the reorganization placed full authority for day-to-day operations in the hands of divisional managers, leaving policy and planning as the Board's responsibility. (Situation) Yet for years the Board has occupied itself largely with short-term operational problems — work that is no longer its to do. (Complication) This raises the question: how should the Board be constituted and organized so that it can perform its proper role? (Question)

**The points the introduction sets out**

1. What the optimum size and composition of the Board should be.
2. How roles and responsibilities should be divided between the Board and the Executive Committee, and how the two should relate to each other.
3. How outside directors can be made effective participants.
4. What principles should govern the selection of directors and their tenure.
5. How the transition from the present arrangement to the desired one should be managed.
