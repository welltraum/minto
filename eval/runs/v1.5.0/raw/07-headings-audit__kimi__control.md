# Audit: Fixture 07 opening

**No governing thought.** The opening states what the memo *describes* ("how project teams identify and deliver significant profit improvements") but never states the memo's point. A Minto opening must deliver the single top-level answer to a question the reader would raise; this one only names the topic.

**No S-C-Q-A introduction.** There is no Situation, no Complication, no reader Question. The reader is not shown why this document exists or what question it answers. "This memorandum describes…" is a statement about the document, not an entry into the reader's mind.

**Announcing structure is not presenting the answer.** Listing six sections ("It is organized in six sections:") tells the reader the document's table of contents, not its key line. The pyramid should lead with the answer and its supporting points, not with a section inventory.

**The headings are topic labels, not assertions.** "Background," "Principles," "Definition," "Organization," "Benefits," "Conditions" name categories of content. Minto headings should carry ideas (action titles) so that reading the headings alone gives the argument's skeleton. From these headings the reader learns what will be covered, nothing about what is being said.

**The setout mixes levels and kinds.** The six items are not parallel: one is context ("Background"), one is a definition ("Definition of project work"), one is a claim ("Distinctive benefits and results"). Points at one level of a pyramid must be of the same logical kind (all steps, all reasons, all problems) and MECE. As listed, they cannot be verified as mutually exclusive or collectively exhaustive, and their mixed types suggest they do not form a coherent key line.

**"Background" as a body section is misplaced.** Under Minto, background belongs in the introduction as the Situation. Elevating it to a section signals the introduction is doing no work.

**Structural ambiguity left unresolved.** The opening implies the memo may be a how-to (process), a rationale (benefits), or a definition. The governing idea would dictate which; with none visible, the groupings cannot be judged for internal coherence — only flagged as suspect.

**Note on scope.** The body is unavailable and has not been inferred. All findings above apply strictly to the visible opening and setout; whether the six sections actually contain grouped, pyramided arguments cannot be assessed.
