The Board should now refocus on policy and planning, leaving day-to-day operations to divisional management.

This memorandum sets out proposals for:

1. The Board’s appropriate size and composition.
2. A clear division of responsibilities between the Board and Executive Committee.
3. Effective participation by outside directors.
4. Principles for director selection and tenure.
5. A practical transition from the current Board and Executive Committee arrangement to the proposed model.
