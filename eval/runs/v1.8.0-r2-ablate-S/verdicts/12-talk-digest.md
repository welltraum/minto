## 5. Observation

The stronger outputs place one causal answer over three or four conclusion-level claims and then attach concrete, attributed, locatable examples to each. The weaker outputs either flatten the talk into many chronological claims, insert topic labels above the real conclusions, exceed the first-level limit, or retain only generic recommendations while losing the examples needed to judge them.
