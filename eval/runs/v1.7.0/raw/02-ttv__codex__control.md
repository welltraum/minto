Subject: Reducing TTW composing costs

TTW should reduce composing costs by simplifying quality control for low-cost titles and by redesigning the composing method to raise productivity.

These actions are warranted because:

1. TTW applies essentially the same quality-control stages to simple novels and complex reference books.  
   - Test selected low-cost titles with fewer or differently timed checks.  
   - Measure effects on quality and customer acceptance.  
   - Savings could reach 10% of total composing cost.

2. TTW’s productivity trails industry benchmarks by 20–50%.  
   - Commission a methods study to identify changes to the composing method.  
   - George Kennedy is willing to test process changes and investigate the productivity gap.

3. Capacity pressure makes improvement urgent.  
   - Manual composition is particularly constrained, most jobs are late, and overtime is more than 50% above budget.  
   - TTW is struggling to recruit and retain compositors after losing two employees.

**Omitted or demoted:** background on interviewees; managers’ disagreement over whether costs are high; comparisons with three other printers; and general statements that composition is a major production cost or that TTW is uncompetitive on simple work. These facts provide context but do not change the recommended actions.
