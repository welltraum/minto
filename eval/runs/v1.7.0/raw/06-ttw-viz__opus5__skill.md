**SCQ ribbon**

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    S["Situation: composition is 40% of hardback and 50-55% of paperback cost"] --> C["Complication: customers call TTW uncompetitive on simple jobs, and TTW does not know whether its cost is excessive"]
    C --> Q["Question: can composing cost be cut?"]
    Q --> A["Answer: yes — substantially, by cutting stages and raising wages"]
    A --> N["Next step: test simple jobs with fewer checks next week, monitoring quality and customer reaction"]
```

**Structure**

- Top: one sentence, answers the reader's question, states a payoff.
- Two first-level groups, same kind (actions), ordered by ranking — the tested, quantified action first.
- Supports: within each branch, evidence of the problem → the action → its payoff.
