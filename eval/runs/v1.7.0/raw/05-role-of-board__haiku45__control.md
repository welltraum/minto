Now that divisional managers have full authority for day-to-day operations, the Board must redefine its role and structure to focus on policy and planning.

To accomplish this, we must address:

1. **The Board's role and responsibilities.** We need to define the broad roles of the Board and Executive Committee, clarify their individual responsibilities, and establish how they relate to each other.

2. **Board composition and governance.** We need to determine the Board's optimal size and composition, establish principles for selecting and tenuring directors, and create mechanisms for effective outside director participation.

3. **The transition approach.** We need to identify alternative ways to move from the present arrangement to the desired governance structure.
