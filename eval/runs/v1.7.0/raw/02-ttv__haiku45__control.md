Subject: TTW Composition Cost Reduction

TTW should conduct two investigations: (1) test whether selected simple jobs can proceed with simplified quality-control processes without compromising quality or customer satisfaction, projected to save 10% of composing costs, and (2) commission a methods study to identify productivity improvements, given that TTW's current productivity trails the industry benchmark by 20-50%.

Composition represents 40% of hardback costs and 50-55% of paperback costs. TTW is uncompetitive on simple work, indicating that composition costs are genuinely excessive. Currently, every title—whether simple or complex—passes through identical quality-control stages.

The simplified process test will measure whether fewer or differently timed quality checks can reduce cost without quality impact. The methods study will address the substantial productivity gap relative to competitors. Department management is willing to investigate both approaches.
