This memorandum proposes restructuring the Board to ensure it focuses on policy and planning by clarifying its role relative to the Executive Committee, optimizing its membership, and establishing a transition plan.

1. Clarify the division of responsibilities between the Board and the Executive Committee.
2. Optimize Board composition by addressing size, selection, tenure, and the effectiveness of outside directors.
3. Evaluate alternatives for transitioning from the current arrangement to the desired structure.
