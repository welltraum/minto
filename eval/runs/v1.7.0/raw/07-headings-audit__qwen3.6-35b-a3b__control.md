**Audit Findings:**

- The opening sentence is descriptive rather than conclusive; it announces the document’s structure instead of stating the main conclusion or answering the reader’s primary question.
- The six headings lack a single controlling idea and are not arranged in a clear hierarchical relationship.
- The headings mix disparate content categories (context, methodology, scope, structure, outcomes, and prerequisites), failing to group related ideas together under common themes.
- The sequence does not follow a recognized logical ordering principle (chronological, structural, degree, or deductive).
- The headings violate MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive); conceptual overlaps exist between items such as “Principles” and “Definition,” and “Organization” and “Conditions.”
- The headings lack parallel grammatical structure, reducing consistency and scannability.
- The setout functions as a table of contents rather than a logical framework that prefigures the argument and guides the reader toward the conclusion.
