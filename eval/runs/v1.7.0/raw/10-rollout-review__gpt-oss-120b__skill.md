**Subject: Approval of Fieldbook Extension to North and Islands in Q1, Contingent on Onboarding and Integration Fixes**

Fieldbook deployment across four regions has delivered measurable operational gains: scheduling density rose from 4.6 to 5.3 jobs per technician per day, average travel time fell 18%, same-day emergency insertion is now automatic (median 4 minutes), and overtime spending dropped 12%. However, rollout defects have eroded these benefits in slower regions and created ongoing rework. Training bottlenecks delayed adoption in South and West, while missing data integrations force manual corrections, obscure equipment history, and distort finance reporting. To secure the projected return on investment in North and Islands, we must close these gaps before expansion. I recommend approving the Q1 extension, provided we restructure onboarding and activate integrations immediately.

**1. Restructure onboarding to eliminate adoption delays**
*   Replace single classroom sessions with continuous onboarding to capture late hires and reduce password-reset tickets, which account for 44% of support volume and concentrate among technicians who missed the initial course.
*   Discontinue shadowing arrangements, which consumed an estimated 380 hours of lost route time and failed to prevent West's lagging adoption (71% after nine weeks, with 34 of 118 technicians never attending any session).
*   **Target:** Replicate Central and East's full adoption within six weeks and restore dispatchers' 100% utilization from week one.

**2. Activate integrations to eliminate rework and billing errors**
*   Evaluate vendor connectors for the legacy asset register and invoicing system; IT confirms both are straightforward and require no additional development, though neither was scoped into the rollout.
*   Implement the vendor's configurable mapping table for job-completion codes to resolve mismatches, stopping roughly 300 weekly manual re-keyings and eliminating transcription errors that cause customer disputes and distort finance reporting by 2–4%.
*   Fix search logic to prevent duplicate customer records (currently 70 per week), restoring complete job histories and removing the burden on support to merge accounts manually.
*   **Target:** Remove technician reliance on back-office calls, restore accurate warranty visibility, and align reporting modules with invoicing. The vendor relationship is strong, and both solutions are ready pending our decision.

**3. Proceed with Q1 extension to North and Islands**
*   Economics remain favorable: monthly licence costs (£31,000 for four regions) are offset by fuel savings (-9%) and overtime reductions (-12%), with Central and East already paying back running costs.
*   Extension adds £13,500 monthly in licences and requires winter travel budgeting; however, the West pattern demonstrates that adoption lags would push new regions underwater for at least two quarters.
*   **Condition:** Extension proceeds only after onboarding and integration fixes are in place to guarantee adoption velocity and data integrity.
