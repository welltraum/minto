**Audit Findings**

1. **Missing Key Line (Answer) in the Introduction**  
   - The opening sentence states the *purpose* of the memorandum (“This memorandum describes…”) rather than presenting the *main conclusion or recommendation* that the reader should take away, as required by the Minto Pyramid Principle.

2. **Improper Structure of the Opening**  
   - The introduction does not follow the “Answer‑Question‑Supporting‑Details” format. It should begin with the *answer* (the core message), followed by a brief *question* (the issue being addressed) and a preview of the supporting points.

3. **Flat List of Headings Lacks Logical Grouping**  
   - The six headings are presented as a simple, unstructured list. Minto recommends arranging headings hierarchically or grouping them into logical clusters that reflect the supporting arguments for the key line.

4. **Parallelism and Consistency Issues**  
   - While most headings are noun phrases, “Background” is a single word that does not match the multi‑word structure of the others, breaking parallelism.

5. **No Explicit Link Between Introduction and Headings**  
   - The introductory sentence does not explicitly tie the *key message* to the six sections that follow, leaving the reader without a clear roadmap of how the sections support the main conclusion.
