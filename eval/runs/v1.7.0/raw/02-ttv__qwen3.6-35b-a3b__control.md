**To:** Engagement Manager / TTW Leadership
**From:** Consultant
**Subject:** Action Plan to Reduce TTW Composing Costs

**Recommendation**
TTW should immediately implement a two-part initiative to reduce composing costs: streamline quality-control procedures for low-complexity titles and conduct a formal methods study to modernize the composing workflow. This approach will directly address current cost inefficiencies, close the productivity gap with industry benchmarks, and alleviate severe capacity constraints.

**Supporting Analysis**
1. **Streamlining quality control for inexpensive titles will generate immediate savings.** 
   Currently, every title—whether a complex reference book or a simple novel—passes through identical quality-assurance stages. Piloting reduced or re-timed checks on selected low-cost jobs is projected to save up to 10% of total composing costs while preserving product quality and customer satisfaction.

2. **A dedicated methods study will resolve the significant productivity deficit.** 
   TTW’s measured output trails industry benchmarks by 20–50%. By systematically evaluating which production stages can be eliminated or resequenced, we can identify targeted efficiency gains. George Kennedy has confirmed his willingness to lead this evaluation and test stage removal.

3. **Process optimization will directly relieve critical capacity bottlenecks.** 
   The department is severely overloaded due to below-market compensation, recent staff attrition, and hiring difficulties. These constraints have caused widespread schedule delays and pushed overtime expenses more than 50% over budget. Reducing unnecessary processing steps will decrease workload pressure, improve on-time delivery, and reduce exposure to pending union wage claims.

**Supplementary Context**
Benchmarking against three peer printers may provide useful industry context, though internal process redesign remains the primary driver of improvement. While management holds differing views on whether current costs are excessive, there is full alignment on proceeding with these investigations.
