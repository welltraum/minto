To align governance with the new operational model—where divisional managers handle day‑to‑day decisions and the Board retains responsibility for policy and planning—we must redesign the Board and Executive Committee. This memorandum therefore focuses on five inter‑related actions:

- Determine the optimal size and composition of the Board.  
- Clarify the distinct roles, responsibilities, and interaction between the Board and the Executive Committee.  
- Enable outside directors to contribute effectively as active participants.  
- Establish principled criteria for selecting directors and defining their tenure.  
- Outline a transition pathway from the current arrangement to the desired governance structure.
