# Audit Result: Headings Structure

## Issues Identified

1. **Missing logical grouping**: The six sections are presented as a simple list rather than organized into mutually supporting groups. Minto's principle requires that headings at the same level answer the same question—the relationship between these headings is unclear.

2. **Absence of top-line logic**: The opening states what the memorandum describes (profit improvements via project teams) but does not establish a clear central conclusion that the sections support. There is no indication of why these six sections—in this arrangement—are the answer to that statement.

3. **Unclear hierarchical relationship**: The sections do not visibly map to a coherent narrative structure. They appear sequentially arranged (Background → Principles → Definition → Organization → Benefits → Conditions) rather than grouped by logical dependency or thematic relationship.

4. **No signposting of section interdependence**: The headings do not suggest how each supports or explains the others, or how they collectively support the memo's stated purpose.

---

**Severity**: The visible structure violates the Minto principle's requirement that heading hierarchies reflect a logical pyramid, with related ideas grouped and prioritized by importance rather than arranged sequentially.
