## Introduction (SCQA)

| Element | Content |
|---|---|
| **Situation** | Composition is 40% of hardback cost and 50–55% of paperback cost. |
| **Complication** | Customers regard TTW as uncompetitive on simple jobs; TTW does not know whether its cost is excessive. |
| **Question** | Can composing cost be reduced? |
| **Answer** | Yes — substantially, by removing unnecessary stages and raising wages. |

**Logical structure:** deductive-free; the two key-line points are a **deductive-of-means** pair — parallel, mutually independent actions that jointly support the governing thought.
