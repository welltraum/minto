The October reorganization gave divisional managers full authority for day-to-day operations, while policy and planning remain the Board's responsibility. Because the Board has spent years dealing with short-term operational problems, its future role and supporting arrangements need to be defined clearly. This memorandum therefore sets out the decisions required to refocus the Board on policy and planning:

1. Determine the Board's optimum size and composition.
2. Define the division of responsibilities between the Board and the Executive Committee.
3. Establish arrangements for effective participation by outside directors.
4. Agree principles for selecting directors and setting their tenure.
5. Plan the transition from the present Board and Executive Committee arrangement to the desired one.
