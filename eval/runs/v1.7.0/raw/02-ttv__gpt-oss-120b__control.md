**To:** Engagement Manager – TTW Project  
**From:** Junior Consultant – Production Cost Review  
**Subject:** Recommendation to Pilot a Simplified Composing Process for Low‑Complexity Titles  

---

### Recommendation  
**Pilot a streamlined composing workflow for low‑complexity (inexpensive) titles and measure its impact on cost and quality.** If the pilot confirms at least a 10 % reduction in composing cost without quality loss, roll the simplified process out to all suitable titles and pursue parallel productivity‑improvement initiatives.

---

### Supporting Arguments  

1. **Composition dominates TT T’s production cost**  
   * Hard‑back titles: ≈ 40 % of total cost  
   * Paperback titles: ≈ 50‑55 % of total cost  

2. **TT T’s composing productivity lags industry benchmarks**  
   * Measured output is 20‑50 % below the best‑practice average for comparable printers.  

3. **Current operating conditions constrain productivity**  
   * Overtime exceeds budget by > 50 %  
   * Staffing shortfall: below‑market pay, recent loss of two compositors, difficulty hiring/retaining staff, resulting in frequent late jobs.  

4. **A focused simplification test offers a realistic cost‑saving opportunity**  
   * Removing or rescheduling non‑essential quality‑control checks on simple titles could cut composing cost by up to 10 % (based on industry case studies).  
   * The pilot can be limited to a representative sample of low‑complexity titles, allowing rapid measurement of cost, cycle‑time, and quality impact.  

5. **Productivity‑improvement initiatives are feasible and necessary**  
   * Benchmark gap (20‑50 %) indicates room for method changes (e.g., automation, workflow redesign).  
   * A separate methods study can be launched after the pilot to target the broader productivity gap.
