Now that divisional managers hold full authority for day-to-day operations, the Board is free to concentrate on the policy and planning role the October reorganization intended for it. Yet the Board continues to spend most of its time on short-term operational problems, as it has for years.

The Board therefore needs to decide how it should organize itself to carry out a policy and planning role. Five questions require decision:

1. How large should the Board be, and who should sit on it?
2. What should the Board do, what should the Executive Committee do, and how should the two relate?
3. How can outside directors be made effective participants rather than observers?
4. What principles should govern the selection of directors and the length of their tenure?
5. What is the best route from the present arrangement to the one we choose?

This memorandum sets out the considerations bearing on each.
