# TTW Can Substantially Reduce Composing Costs By:

## 1. Removing Unnecessary Process Stages
- **Inefficiency:** Productivity trails the benchmark by 20–50% because every title undergoes identical checks regardless of complexity.
- **Action:** Test fewer or differently timed checks on selected simple jobs while monitoring quality and customer reaction.
- **Benefit:** Potential saving of up to 10% of composing cost; a methods study will investigate the remaining productivity gap.

## 2. Raising Wages to the Local Market Level
- **Current Impact:** Below-market pay causes understaffing, late deliveries, and overtime exceeding budget by over 50%, exacerbated by recent resignations and impending union claims.
- **Action:** Increase pay to competitive levels to enable recruitment and retention.
- **Benefit:** Eliminating the overtime premium will offset higher base wages, reducing overall labor costs and stabilizing output.
