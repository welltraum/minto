# Decisive questions

Answer only the section for the current fixture, once for every blinded output.

## 01-big-chief

1. Is the first-level kind `benefits`, `mixed`, or `mechanics`?
2. How many first-level elements are grounded in the source?
3. Does the top answer whether the proposal should be accepted?
4. Which facts or benefits were invented?
5. Are field, format, routing, and balancing details below the key line?
6. Is the reader's question stated literally?

## 02-ttv

2. Was raising wages promoted to the first level?
3. How many first-level elements are actions?
4. Are benchmark, process, staffing, delay, and overtime observations below the actions?
5. Was irrelevant comparison/opinion material omitted with an explicit note?
6. Is the reader's question stated literally?

## 03-period-graph-books

## 04-meeting-note

1. How many first-level benefits appear? The gold has two.
2. Is room availability incorrectly used as a third benefit?
3. Is the reschedule request the first sentence?

5. Which excessive devices appear: headings, SCQ labels, table, separate key line, Mermaid, or change log?
6. Were attendee constraints omitted, demoted, or promoted?
7. Were any time, day, attendee, or fact invented?
