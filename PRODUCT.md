# Minto

## Summary

Minto is an installable Codex and Claude Code plugin that applies the Minto Pyramid Principle to business writing and thinking. It turns vague material into a reader question, an answer-first top line, a coherent set of supporting ideas, and a structure that is easy to act on.

## Audience

The primary audience is knowledge workers who use AI to prepare consequential communication: product and engineering leaders, consultants, operators, founders, and executives. They arrive with notes, drafts, or an unclear communication goal and need a result that a busy reader can understand quickly.

## Problem

General-purpose assistants can improve prose while leaving the underlying argument weak: the decision remains buried, first-level ideas mix different kinds, evidence is invented or dropped, and headings describe topics instead of conclusions.

## Value Proposition

Minto makes AI-assisted business writing decision-ready by forcing the reader's question, the answer, the kind and order of supporting ideas, and evidence gaps to become explicit before polishing the prose.

## Capabilities

- `intent` clarifies the reader, decision, complication, answer, and missing evidence.
- `audit` diagnoses pyramid-logic defects without rewriting the source.
- `write` restructures notes or drafts into answer-first communication.
- `viz` renders the pyramid and SCQ flow, with an optional shareable HTML view.

## Success Criteria

- A first-time visitor understands the promise in one screen and can install Minto without opening the README.
- Codex and Claude Code installation paths are equally clear and copyable.
- The site demonstrates the method with real structures and original examples, not abstract feature claims.
- Benchmark evidence is presented accurately, including mixed results.
- Both English and Russian versions are complete, accessible, fast, and usable without JavaScript.

## Principles

1. Lead with the answer, then expose the logic beneath it.
2. Show structure through real examples and diagrams.
3. Prefer measured claims to promotional language.
4. Make installation the shortest path through the page.
5. Preserve facts and name uncertainty instead of filling gaps.

## Voice

Decisive, architectural, and exact. Sentences are short, concrete, and free of hype. The page should feel like a carefully annotated plan pinned up before an executive review.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, high text contrast, semantic landmarks, and equivalent English and Russian content. Core content and installation instructions must remain available without JavaScript.
