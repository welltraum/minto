**Subject: onboarding and retention in the mobile app**

As you know, we did a great deal of product work last quarter, shipped two
releases, added four people to the team, and generally made good progress on
features. I also want to recognize the design team and note that we finally
closed the authentication debt we had discussed since January.

There is more to say about the metrics. D7 retention fell, first-purchase
conversion also declined, and traffic did not change. Support has received
complaints about registration. Engineering says that logic has needed a rewrite
for some time. We also lack step-level funnel analytics, so we cannot locate the
drop-off precisely. Seasonality, a competitor promotion, and a two-week push
notification outage may also have contributed.

We discussed several directions: improving onboarding, revisiting price,
improving the first screen, communicating with users, and improving general
quality and speed. We should examine all of them carefully. Another option is
to hire an analyst, because without the data we are guessing.

Support received 340 contacts this month, roughly one-third about registration.
I believe we should rewrite the registration screen in the next sprint.

Overall, I propose that we focus next quarter on onboarding: first add funnel
analytics, then redesign registration and first launch, and return to pricing
afterward. I need your decision on priority and the analyst hire by Friday.
