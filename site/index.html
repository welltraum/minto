Four modes. One discipline.

Minto routes the request by what you need now: clarify the intent, diagnose the logic, rebuild the writing, or expose the structure.

intent
Find the reader’s real question and the one-sentence answer before drafting.
audit
Mark structural defects, score the logic, and prescribe fixes without rewriting.
write
Turn notes into an answer-first memo, update, email, one-pager, or deck storyline.
viz
Render the pyramid and SCQ flow in Mermaid or an optional shareable page.
