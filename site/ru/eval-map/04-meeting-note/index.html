Эталон судьи gold.md

Gold 04: meeting note

Source structure: Minto (2010), Preface.

Expected structure

Top: Request approval to move today's meeting to Thursday at 11:00.

Key line has exactly two reader benefits:

  1. The time is more convenient for Collins and Johnson.
  2. Clifford can attend.

The room's availability is a constraint, not a third reader benefit. Do not add
a third branch just to satisfy a numerical norm.

Proportionality

The result should remain a two-sentence note, no longer than the source. Section
headings, an SCQ template, tables, a separate key-line block, or a Mermaid
diagram are excessive. Omitting the detailed scheduling constraints is
acceptable when the answer and both benefits remain clear.
